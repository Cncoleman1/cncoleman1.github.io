I was selected for two seperate research experiences for undergraduates in for 8 weeks in 2021 at Cornell University and in 2022 at the University of Georgia (UGA).

![Cornell REU](/assets/img/portfolio/Cornell-REU.png)
At Cornell, my mentor was Dr. Cedric Feschotte. The purpose of the study was to understand the driving force behind zinc finger expansions in metazoans. A correlation between the number of Long Terminal Repeat retroelements  (LTRs) and the number of zinc fingers found in that organism’s genome was found by Thomas and Schneider in 2011. To test this correlation across 2917 metazoan species, I designed a bioinformatics pipeline that would collect the number of zinc fingers and the number of transposable elements in a given genome.

![UGA REU](/assets/img/portfolio/UGA-REU-Lab.png)
At UGA, my mentor was Dr. Nikki Shariat.  My project was to assess *Salmonella*
contamination in four first and second order creek systems. I worked with graduate students Jared Smith and Amy Siceloff to sample 19 sites in May, June, and July of 2022. I used Real-time PCR (qPCR) was used to perform
microbial source tracking and characterize sources of fecal contamination.
