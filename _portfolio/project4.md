I worked in the Summer of 2023 as a Residential Counselor and Barnard College in New York City, New York and in Summer of 2024 at the University of Pennsylvania in Philadelpia, Pennsylvania. I mentored over high school sophomores living on-campus for the summer and attending pre-college programs at either Barnard or UPenn. As a recent graduate, I shared my experience in undergrad with scientific research, getting involved on-campus, and applying for national awards/fellowships. 

![NY-PA Selfie](/assets/img/portfolio/NY-PA-Selfie.png)

---
I also supported social events for students to connnect with each other such as sunrise yoga, spa nights and ice cream socials. I navigated both large cities to support group excursions to a variety of museums and historical sites.

During Fall 2023/Spring 2024 I taught STEM concepts to preschoolers as a STEM Specialist at Amazing Explorers Academy. I facilitated activities, crafts, and collaborative projects that helped foster a strong sense of curiosity and interest for each of my classes.
